Use an android phone preferably, connected to PC via USB cable. If asked for a connection type, choose ‘File Transfer’
Install ‘Unity Remote’’ on your phone.
In phone settings, search ‘’about device’, and tap the device name 7 times to enable developer mode.(may be different depending on what phone you use)
In Phone settings, search ‘debugging’, and enable ‘USB Debugging’
In Unity, Change ‘game’ to simulator, and choose a resolution similar to your phone(we used ‘Punch Hole Center 1440 x 3088’)
In Unity, In Build Settings, change from ‘Windows’’ to ‘Android’(or Iphone), and press switch
In unity, Project Settings, Editor, change device from ‘none’ to ‘any android device’(or your phone if it shows up)
In Unity, Project settings, player, ,change input system to both, since it uses the old input system
Have ‘Unity Remote’’ open when starting the game
